ABOUT IUCDA

Connecting our people. Building our future.

Ikot Ukap Itam Community Development Association provides a platform for community members to share information, participate in development initiatives and strengthen relationships across families and generations.

This website is designed to serve residents, indigenes and members of the community living outside Ikot Ukap Itam.

Our guiding values

Unity: Encourage cooperation, respect and togetherness.

Heritage: Preserve our history, identity and cultural values.

Development: Support transparent, inclusive and sustainable community progress.

Service: Promote responsible leadership and participation.
