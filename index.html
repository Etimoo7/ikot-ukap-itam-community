ABOUT IUCDA

Connecting our people. Building our future.

Ikot Ukap Itam is a proud community in Itu Local Government Area, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria.

The Ikot Ukap Itam Community Development Association provides a platform for community members to share information, participate in development initiatives and strengthen relationships across families and generations.

This website serves residents, indigenes and members of the community living outside Ikot Ukap Itam.

Our Guiding Values

Unity: Encourage cooperation, respect and togetherness.

Heritage: Preserve our history, identity and cultural values.

Development: Support sustainable community progress.

Service: Promote responsible leadership and participation.
